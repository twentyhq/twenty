# LinkedIn

**Your LinkedIn conversations, on the people and companies they're about.**

## ✨ What you get

- **LinkedIn messages in Twenty**: every message you send or receive lands on the matching Person and Company, next to their emails
- **Your history too**: past conversations are imported when you connect, either all of them or only those with people already in Twenty
- **Private by default, shareable**: choose whether teammates see your messages or only who you talked to and when
- **Matched on the LinkedIn field, then by name**: a conversation links to the Person whose LinkedIn profile URL matches, or to the only Person with the same name, whose LinkedIn field is then filled in
- **People created for you**: people you message (or also those who message you) become People records, like with email
- **One connection per member**: each teammate connects their own LinkedIn account in a few clicks

## 💳 Billing

**$10 per month** per connected account.

## 🚀 Get started

1. Install **LinkedIn** from **Settings → Applications**.
2. Each member opens **Settings → App preferences → LinkedIn**, clicks **Add connection** and signs in to LinkedIn.

## 📌 Heads up

- **Read-only for now.** Messages are imported as they happen; reply from LinkedIn. Marking chats as read on LinkedIn is not available yet.
- **Namesakes are not guessed.** When several People share a participant's name, nobody is linked: add the LinkedIn URL to the right Person and their messages link to it.
- **Settings are per member.** Import, visibility, contact auto-creation and people matching live in **Settings → Applications → LinkedIn** and apply to your own connection.
