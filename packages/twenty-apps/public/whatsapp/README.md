# WhatsApp

**Your WhatsApp conversations, on the people and companies they're about.**

## ✨ What you get

- **WhatsApp messages in Twenty**: every message you send or receive lands on the Person with that phone number and on their Company, next to their emails
- **Shared files included**: images, videos, voice notes and documents up to 25 MB are attached to their message
- **Replies from Twenty**: answer a WhatsApp conversation from its thread in Twenty, sent from your own WhatsApp account
- **Linked like WhatsApp Desktop**: each teammate scans a QR code with their phone, no password to type

## 💳 Billing

**$7 per month** per connected WhatsApp account. Billing follows the number of connected accounts, so disconnecting one stops its charge.

## 🚀 Get started

1. Install **WhatsApp** from **Settings → Applications**.
2. Each member opens **Settings → App preferences → WhatsApp** and clicks **Add connection**: a QR code shows up.
3. On their phone, they open WhatsApp → **Settings → Linked devices → Link a device** and scan the code.

## 📌 Heads up

- **People are matched on their phone number.** A message links to the Person whose **Phones** field holds the sender's number, calling code included. Add the number to a Person and their messages link to it.
- **Hidden numbers are left out.** WhatsApp does not always share a participant's phone number, mostly in groups. Those participants are skipped, and a chat where nobody's number is known is not imported.
- **Replies go to existing conversations.** You can reply from Twenty to any imported conversation, with files of up to 25 MB each once message attachments are enabled on your workspace. Starting a new conversation happens on WhatsApp.
- **Only recent history is imported.** **Sync now** imports your most recent conversations: pick 20 (default), 50 or 100 in **Past conversations**, or **None** to skip history and only receive new messages. Right after linking, WhatsApp is still handing over your history, so a first sync can miss older conversations.
- **You can pick the conversations to import.** In **Import**, choose **Selected chats** and tick the conversations Twenty should bring in: only those are imported, new messages and history alike, whoever is in them. The list shows your 200 most recent conversations at most, and up to 200 can be selected.
- **Group chats need a contact.** A group is imported once at least one member is already a Person in Twenty.
- **A group conversation shows on every member.** Each message of a group is linked to all its members whose number is known, so the conversation appears on the record of each of them. When contact auto-creation applies, a Person is created for every one of those members; a member WhatsApp only knows by their number is named after that number. Run **Sync now** again to link the members of groups imported earlier.
- **Keep your phone online.** WhatsApp unlinks devices of a phone that stays offline for too long; the connection then asks to be reconnected with a new QR code.
- **Settings are per member.** Import, visibility, contact auto-creation and read status live in **Settings → App preferences → WhatsApp → Sync** and apply to your own connection.
