# Granola

**Your Granola meeting summaries and transcripts, right inside your CRM.**

## ✨ What you get

- **Granola summaries and speaker transcripts saved to Call Recordings**
- **Calendar links for clear matches**
- **New and edited notes synced as they happen**
- **Your last 31 days imported when you connect, and up to 3,650 days on demand**
- **Every folder, or up to 100 you pick**
- **Built for AI & automation**

## 💳 Billing

**Free to run**: no per-seat, per-note or per-import charge. AI actions use AI
credits on the model's token usage.

## 📌 Heads up

- **Needs Granola Business or Enterprise.** Create an API key in Granola under
  **Settings → Connectors → API keys** and paste it in the app's **Settings**
  tab.
- **One key syncs for the whole workspace.** Every member with access to Call
  Recordings sees what that key can reach. Use a workspace key to cover your
  team; a personal key syncs only what its owner can see.
- **Summaries and transcripts only.** Granola shares no audio or video, and
  private scratchpad notes are never imported.
- **Deletes don't sync.** Deleting a note in Granola keeps its Twenty recording,
  and a recording you delete in Twenty stays deleted.

**Self-hosting?** Live sync needs a public HTTPS server URL: see the
[setup guide](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-apps/public/granola/SETUP.md).
