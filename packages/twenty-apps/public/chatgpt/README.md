# ChatGPT

Connect ChatGPT to Twenty to work with your records from a conversation.

- Each member connects their own ChatGPT account.
- ChatGPT acts with that member's permissions.

## How to connect

Open the app in the Twenty marketplace and select **Connect**. ChatGPT opens on the Twenty app; sign in to Twenty and approve the access it asks for.

This app installs nothing in your workspace.
