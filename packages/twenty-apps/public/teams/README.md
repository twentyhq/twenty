# Teams

**Your Microsoft Teams meeting transcripts and AI summaries, right inside your CRM. Ask Twenty about your CRM from any Teams chat.**

## ✨ What you get

- **Teams transcripts saved to Call Recordings**
- **Microsoft transcripts synced in your workspace**
- **AI summaries**
- **A chat assistant in Teams**: ask questions about your CRM and create or
  update records from a message.

## 📌 How chat works

Message the Twenty bot in a personal chat, or mention it in a channel or group
chat. It looks up records in your workspace, makes the changes you ask for and
replies in the conversation.

It answers as the workspace member whose email matches your Microsoft account,
with that member's permissions, so it can't see or change anything they
couldn't in Twenty. If no member matches, it replies with a short notice
instead.

> Answers are generated by AI and can be inaccurate. Check important details in
> Twenty before acting on them.

- **Ask questions**: look up a person, a company or your open opportunities.
- **Create and update records**: people, companies, opportunities, notes and
  tasks, from a message.
- **Confirmation before deleting**: the bot lists what it would delete and
  waits for you to confirm.

## 📌 What you can ask

- "How many open opportunities are in the pipeline?"
- "What deals are closing this month?"
- "What do we know about Acme Corp?"
- "Create a company called Acme Corp"
- "Create a task to follow up on my newest lead"

Send `help` to see examples in Teams.

## 💳 Billing

**Free.** Optional AI summaries and chat assistant answers use AI credits.

## 📌 Getting started

1. Install **Teams** from **Settings → Applications**.
2. Open **Settings → Applications → Teams → Settings**, select
   **Add connection** and sign in with your Microsoft work or school account.
   Your organization may require admin consent when connecting.

**For transcripts**

3. Have your Microsoft 365 admin enable **Microsoft Graph access** under
   **Teams admin center → Meetings → Meeting settings → Transcript API access**.
   Enable **Include speaker attribution** there to import speaker names too.

**For chat**

3. Turn on **Enable chat** in **Settings → Applications → Teams → Settings**.
   Chat needs the workspace-shared connection from step 2; a personal
   connection is not enough.
4. Have a Teams admin upload the Twenty app package in **Teams admin center →
   Teams apps → Manage apps** and allow it for the users who need it.
5. Message the bot in a personal chat, or mention it in a channel or group
   chat. Microsoft accounts with the same email as a workspace member are
   linked automatically. Members who can manage roles can link the rest under
   **Linked Teams accounts** in the app's settings.

**Self-hosting?** Your admin registers the Microsoft app, sets up the bot and
configures a public HTTPS server URL first: see the
[setup guide](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-apps/public/teams/SETUP.md).

## 📌 Heads up

- **Work or school accounts, and meetings you organize.** Personal Microsoft
  accounts and meetings where you are only an attendee are not supported.
- **Imports are shared with your workspace.**
- **No audio or video.** Only transcripts and their summaries are imported.
- **The bot only reads messages addressed to it**: personal chat messages, and
  channel or group messages that mention it.
- **One workspace per Microsoft organization for chat.** The first workspace to
  add a workspace-shared connection for an organization gets its chat
  messages.
- **Chat supports Microsoft 365 commercial tenants.** GCC is untested. GCC High
  and DoD are not supported.
