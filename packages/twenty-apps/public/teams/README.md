# Microsoft Teams

**Your Microsoft Teams meeting transcripts and AI summaries, right inside your CRM.**

## ✨ What you get

- **Teams transcripts saved to Call Recordings**
- **Microsoft transcripts synced in your workspace**
- **AI summaries**

## 💳 Billing

**Free**

## 📌 Getting started

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-apps/public/teams/SETUP.md).

## 📌 Heads up

- **Work or school accounts, and meetings you organize.** Personal Microsoft
  accounts and meetings where you are only an attendee are not supported.
- **Imports are shared with your workspace.**
- **Transcripts and summaries only.** Audio and video are not imported.
