# Telegram

**Your CRM, in a Telegram chat. Ask Twenty anything in a direct message with the bot.**

## How it works

Message the bot and it looks up records in your workspace, makes the changes
you ask for and replies in the chat. It only answers people whose Telegram
account is connected to a Twenty user, and it acts with that user's
permissions, so it can't see or change anything they couldn't in Twenty.

> Answers are generated by AI and can be inaccurate. Check important details in
> Twenty before acting on them.

## What you can ask

- "How many open opportunities do we have?"
- "Which deals are closing this month?"
- "Create a company called Acme Corp"
- "Add a note to Jane Doe: follow up on pricing next week"

## Connecting your account

One Telegram bot serves the whole Twenty server. A member connects their own
Telegram account from Twenty, and the bot then knows which workspace and which
member a direct message comes from.

1. In Twenty, run **Connect Telegram** from the command menu to get a one-time
   link. Admins can also start from the Telegram app settings.
2. The link opens the bot in Telegram. Pressing **Start** completes the
   connection. On a computer without the Telegram app, scan the QR code shown
   under the link with your phone, or send the `/start` command shown there to
   the bot.
3. Sending `/disconnect` to the bot, blocking the bot, or disconnecting from the
   app settings removes it. Members with the roles permission can disconnect
   any account there.

A connection link works once and expires after 10 minutes.

## Heads up

- **Direct messages only.** The bot ignores groups and channels.
- **Photos and PDFs.** The bot reads one photo or PDF per message, up to
  10 MB, with or without a caption. Other files and voice messages are not
  read.
- **Recent context only.** The bot remembers the last 10 questions of a chat.
- **Slow answers arrive late.** After about three and a half minutes the bot
  says it is still working, then sends the answer when the run ends, for up to
  30 minutes.
- **Answers can be rated.** Each answer has a thumbs up and a thumbs down
  button. The rating is saved on the request and can be changed.
- **Requests are saved.** Each question and its answer is kept as a Telegram
  Assistant Request in the workspace.
- **One workspace per Telegram account.** An account connected to one
  workspace has to send `/disconnect` before it can be connected to another
  workspace on the same server.
- **Nothing is stored for strangers.** People who message the bot without a
  connected account get a fixed reply, and their message is not saved.

## Getting started

Your server admin creates the bot and sets its credentials first: see the

## Billing

**Free to run**: no per-seat or per-message charge. Answers use AI credits on
the model's token usage.
