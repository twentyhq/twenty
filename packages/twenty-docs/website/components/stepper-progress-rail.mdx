## Props

{/* Generated by scripts/docs/render-api-reference.mjs from component-docs.json. Do not edit by hand. */}

<ParamField body="activeStepIndex" type="number" required>
  The current step, counted from 0. Only its pill fills.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="localProgress" type="number" required>
  How far the active step has gone, from 0 to 1: its pill fills to that share. Values outside are clamped.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="stepCount" type="number" required>
  How many pills the rail draws, one per step.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="inactiveColor" type="string">
  Color of the pills that are not active, through `--rail-inactive`. Without it, the rail keeps its own color.
</ParamField>
