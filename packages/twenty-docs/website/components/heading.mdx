## Usage guidelines

<Check>Write one translatable string: `*span*`switches to the accent family and a newline is a break; no `br` (heading-notation.ts:6-12)</Check>

<Check>The accent swaps family: a serif heading gets a sans accent, and the reverse (Heading.tsx:75-82)</Check>

<Check>`tracking="normal"`is for a quote set at heading size (Heading.tsx:32-37)</Check>

<ParamField body="as" type="&quot;h1&quot; | &quot;h2&quot; | &quot;h3&quot; | &quot;h4&quot; | &quot;h5&quot; | &quot;h6&quot;">
  The heading level, `h1` to `h6`. Independent of `size`: pick the level for the outline, the size for the look.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="className" type="string">
  Extra class, merged after the component's own.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="family" type="&quot;serif&quot; | &quot;sans&quot;" default="serif">
  `serif` or `sans`. Accent spans (`*text*` in the string) switch to the other family.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="size" type="&quot;xl&quot; | &quot;lg&quot; | &quot;md&quot; | &quot;sm&quot; | &quot;xs&quot;" default="md">
  The step on the heading type ramp, `headingXl` to `headingXs`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="tracking" type="&quot;tight&quot; | &quot;normal&quot;" default="tight">
  `tight` keeps the family's tightened letter-spacing. `normal` resets it, for a quote set at heading size.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="weight" type="&quot;light&quot; | &quot;regular&quot; | &quot;medium&quot;" default="regular">
  A `FONT_WEIGHT` token.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="wrap" type="&quot;normal&quot; | &quot;balance&quot;" default="balance">
  `balance` evens out the line lengths (`text-wrap: balance`), `normal` lets lines fill.
</ParamField>
