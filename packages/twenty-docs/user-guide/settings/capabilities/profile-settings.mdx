## Personal Information

### Name and Email
- **Display Name**: Update how your name appears to other workspace members
- **Email Address**: Change your login email (requires verification)
- **Profile Picture**: Upload a custom avatar or use your initials

## Security Settings

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Enable 2FA to add an extra layer of security to your account:
1. Go to **Settings → Profile**
2. Click **Authenticator app**
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app
4. Enter the verification code to confirm

### Lost Your Authenticator
If you can't access your authenticator app, ask a workspace admin for a recovery code. Then:
1. Sign in with your email and password
2. On the verification screen, click **Lost your authenticator? Use a recovery code**
3. Enter the recovery code and click **Continue**

Your current authenticator for that workspace is removed and your other sessions are signed out. Set up two-factor authentication again from **Settings → Profile**.

### Password Management
- **Change Password**: Update your current password
- **Password Requirements**: Must be at least 8 characters long

## Profile Management

### Delete Account
<Warning>
Deleting your account will permanently remove your access to all workspaces. This action cannot be undone. You'll lose access to all workspaces where you're a member. If you only want to exit specific teams, consider leaving individual workspaces instead.
</Warning>

To delete your account:
1. Go to **Settings → Profile**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Delete Account**
4. Confirm by typing your email address
