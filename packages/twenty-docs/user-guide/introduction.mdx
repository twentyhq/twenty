## Make Twenty fit your team

Twenty is built to change with your business. This guide shows you how to set up your workspace and shape it as you go, from the data you track to the screens your team works in every day.

- **Set up your data.** Model objects and fields around your business, then bring in your records from CSV files or another CRM.
- **Bring your conversations in.** Sync email and calendar so every exchange lands on the right record, and reach your contacts with email campaigns.
- **Organize the work.** Build the views, pipelines and record pages your team uses every day.
- **Automate the busywork.** Hand repetitive steps to workflows and multi-step tasks to AI agents.
- **Stay in control.** Follow your numbers on dashboards, decide who sees what with roles, and manage billing and settings.
