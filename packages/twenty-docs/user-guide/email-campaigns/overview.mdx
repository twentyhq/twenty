## Campaigns for sales teams

<Warning>
**Beta Feature**: Email campaigns are in beta. Contact us via chat to enable them for your workspace.
</Warning>

Your sales team already works from Twenty. Your accounts, people, pipeline, activity, and custom data are here. Campaigns lets you act on that data without exporting it to a separate marketing tool.

Campaigns are one-to-many. You create one email for a focused list, not a separate draft for every lead. Fields such as a person's first name can still be filled in when the campaign sends.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/email-campaigns/campaigns-list.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

Use campaigns to run sales plays that would be hard to manage one email at a time:

- **Work target accounts.** Reach the right people at companies in a specific industry, region, size, or account tier.
- **Move stalled deals.** Send a useful follow-up to people linked to opportunities that have been sitting too long.
- **Run event and outbound plays.** Invite the people who match your ideal customer profile, not every contact in your database.
- **Grow existing accounts.** Reach customers based on their plan, usage, renewal date, or any custom field your team tracks.

## Use the CRM work you have already done

### Build better segments

Build a list from the data your team already maintains: account owner, deal stage, industry, last activity, product plan, lead score, or your own custom fields. No CSV export. No upload. No second contact database to keep in sync.

### See the signal, then act on it

Use custom dashboards, charts, and funnels to spot where revenue is getting stuck. Then build a focused campaign around that signal. Campaigns turn a report your team sees into a next step your team can take.

### Keep the work moving with workflows

Workflows can create and update Lists and List Members as records change. For example, add new people who match your ideal customer profile to an outbound list, or move contacts into a follow-up list when a deal reaches a certain stage. Your campaign audience can keep up with the way your sales team works.

See [Workflows](/user-guide/workflows/overview) to build automations around your sales process.

### Use AI when it saves time

Ask Twenty's AI to find the right people, make a list, or draft one campaign for a list. It uses the CRM data it can access and follows your workspace permissions. It prepares the work; your team reviews it and decides whether to send.

See [Create a campaign with AI](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/create-a-campaign-with-ai) for the full workflow.

### Keep results close to the work

After you send, the campaign shows who received it, what bounced, what was skipped, and whether anyone marked it as spam. Use that information when you plan the next play.

## Send from your own domain

Campaigns come from an address on your domain, so people know who sent them. Twenty walks you through domain verification and puts the unsubscribe page on your domain too.

Your workspace can send up to 1,000 campaign emails a day. Need a higher limit? Contact us via chat. We review each request and raise limits case by case.
