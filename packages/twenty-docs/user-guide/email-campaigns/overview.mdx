Email campaigns let you send one email to a list of people, personalized for each of them, from an address on your own domain. Because campaigns live in your CRM, your audience is built from the records you already have, and the results stay attached to them.

  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

## How it works

1. **Verify your domain.** Add a few DNS records so mailbox providers trust email from your domain.
2. **Build a list.** Pick the people who should receive the campaign.
3. **Write the email.** Use the editor, with variables such as each person's first name, or let the AI write it for you.
4. **Test and send.** Send yourself a test, then send now or schedule for later.
5. **Follow the results.** See how many emails were delivered, bounced or reported as spam.

## Use AI

Twenty's AI can build your audience from your CRM data and write the whole campaign, from the subject line to the layout and personalization. You review the draft and click send. See [Create a campaign with AI](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/create-a-campaign-with-ai).

## Protection

- **Unsubscribe links** on every campaign, with one-click unsubscribe in Gmail, Yahoo and other email apps.
- **Topics**, so recipients can opt out of one kind of email and keep the rest.
- **Automatic suppression** of addresses that bounce, report spam or unsubscribe.

## Limits

| Limit                   | Value                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recipients per campaign | 10,000                                                                                                  |
| Test emails             | 3 per workspace per day                                                                                 |
| Cost                    | Each email sent uses credits, on Twenty Cloud. See [Credits](/user-guide/billing/capabilities/credits). |
