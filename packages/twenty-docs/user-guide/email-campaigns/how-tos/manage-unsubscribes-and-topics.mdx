---
title: Manage Unsubscribes and Topics
description: See who opted out, and let recipients choose which emails they keep receiving.
---

Every campaign includes an unsubscribe link, and Twenty keeps track of who used it. Topics go one step further: they let people opt out of one kind of email, like a newsletter, while still receiving the rest.

## How unsubscribing works

Every campaign email includes:

- A line in the footer: "Don't want these emails? Unsubscribe."
- The one-click unsubscribe headers that Gmail, Yahoo and other email apps use to show their own **Unsubscribe** button.

Clicking the link opens an unsubscribe page on your own domain, for example `unsubscribe.yourcompany.com`:

- **Without public topics**, the page offers a single **Unsubscribe** button.
- **With public topics**, recipients see one checkbox per topic. They untick what they no longer want and click **Update**, or click **Unsubscribe all**.

An unsubscribe takes effect immediately. The person is skipped on every later campaign it applies to.

<Note>
  The unsubscribe button built into Gmail, Yahoo and other email apps
  unsubscribes the person from all your campaigns, not only the topic of the
  email they received.
</Note>

## Part 1: Create topics

Topics are categories of email, such as **Newsletter**, **Product updates** or **Events**.

1. Go to **Settings → Communication**.
2. Under **Unsubscribe**, click **Manage unsubscribe**.
3. Open the **Topics** tab and click **Add topic**.
4. In **Name**, type the name recipients see, for example `Newsletter`.
5. In **Description**, optionally note what the emails contain, for your team's reference.
6. Turn on **Listed on the unsubscribe page** to make the topic public.
7. Click **Save**.

| Visibility  | Behavior                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public**  | Recipients see the topic on the unsubscribe page and can opt out of it on its own.                    |
| **Private** | Recipients never see it. Use it to group campaigns for your own tracking. It still respects opt-outs. |

New topics are private until you turn on **Listed on the unsubscribe page**.

## Part 2: Use a topic in a campaign

In the campaign's **Email** tab, choose the topic under **Unsubscribe topic**. The field appears once at least one topic exists.

When the campaign sends, Twenty skips:

- People who unsubscribed from this topic.
- People who unsubscribed from everything.

People who only opted out of a different topic still receive it.

## Part 3: See who unsubscribed

1. Go to **Settings → Communication**.
2. Under **Unsubscribe**, click **Manage unsubscribe**.
3. Open the **Unsubscribers** tab.

The list shows every address that no longer receives campaigns:

| Column            | What it shows                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email address** | The address that is skipped.                                                                                  |
| **Scope**         | **All emails**, or the name of the topic they left.                                                           |
| **Reason**        | **Unsubscribed**, **Bounce** (the address does not exist) or **Complaint** (they reported the email as spam). |
| **Date**          | When it happened.                                                                                             |

Use the search and the filters to find a specific address, reason or topic.

  src="/images/user-guide/email-campaigns/unsubscribers-tab.png"

<Warning>
  Bounces and spam complaints are permanent. Sending to those addresses again
  would harm your domain's reputation, so Twenty does not allow it.
</Warning>

## Preview the unsubscribe page

The **Unsubscribe page** tab shows exactly what recipients see, with your current public topics. Nothing is saved when you click through the preview.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Recipients only see an Unsubscribe button, not my topics">
  Only public topics appear on the unsubscribe page. Open the topic and turn on
  **Listed on the unsubscribe page**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Someone unsubscribed from one topic but stopped receiving everything">
  They probably used the unsubscribe button built into their email app, which
  unsubscribes them from all your campaigns. Only the link in the email footer
  offers the choice of topics.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A person was skipped but they want to receive emails again">
  If you have public topics, they can open the unsubscribe link in an email
  received in the last 30 days, tick the topics they want and click **Update**.
  Bounces and spam complaints cannot be undone.
</Accordion>
