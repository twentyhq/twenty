---
title: Send Your First Campaign
description: Build a list, write the email, test it, and send or schedule it.
---

A campaign sends one email to everyone in a list, personalized for each person. You build the list, write the email, send yourself a test, then send it now or schedule it for later.

<Tip>
  The AI can do most of this for you: build the list and write the whole email.
  See [Create a campaign with
  AI](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/create-a-campaign-with-ai).
</Tip>

## Before you start

You need three things:

- **A verified sending domain.** See [Set up a sending domain](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/set-up-a-sending-domain).
- **The Workspace settings permission**, to send, test and schedule campaigns.
- **Email credits**, on Twenty Cloud. Each email sent uses credits. See [Credits](/user-guide/billing/capabilities/credits).

## Part 1: Build a list

A list is a hand-picked group of people who will receive a campaign.

1. Press `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows) and search for **Go to Lists**.
2. Click **New List** and give the list a name, for example `Newsletter subscribers`.
3. Open the list and use the **Members** field to add people.

You can also add a single person from their record, using the **Lists** field.

<Note>
  Twenty sends to each person's primary email address. People without an email
  address are skipped, and so is anyone who has unsubscribed, bounced or
  reported spam.
</Note>

## Part 2: Create the campaign

1. Press `Cmd+K` and choose **Compose Campaign**, or open **Campaigns** and click **New Campaign**.
2. Give the campaign a name. Only your team sees it.
3. Open the **Email** tab and fill in the envelope:
   - **From**: the address the campaign comes from. It lists the addresses of your email channels.
   - **To**: the list you built in Part 1. You can also create a new list here.
   - **Unsubscribe topic**: optional. Choose a topic so that people can opt out of this kind of email only. This field appears once you have created a topic. See [Manage unsubscribes and topics](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/manage-unsubscribes-and-topics).
   - **Subject**: the subject line recipients see.

## Part 3: Write the email

Type your email in the editor below the subject. Twenty saves your draft as you go.

- Type `/` to see commands, or use the rail on the left to insert **Text**, **Image**, **Blocks** and **Variables**.
- **Blocks** include **Section**, **2 Columns**, **3 Columns**, **Button**, **Divider**, **Image** and **HTML**, for pasting your own HTML.

### Personalize with variables

Variables are replaced with each person's own details when the campaign sends. Insert them from **Variables**, or type them in double curly braces:

| Variable             | Replaced with |
| -------------------- | ------------- |
| `{{name.firstName}}` | First name    |
| `{{name.lastName}}`  | Last name     |
| `{{fullName}}`       | Full name     |
| `{{jobTitle}}`       | Job title     |

Any other text, number, date or select field on People is available too, including your custom fields. Variables work in the subject line, the body and link addresses.

For example, `Hi {{name.firstName}},` becomes `Hi Ana,` for Ana.

<Warning>
  If a person has no value for a variable, it is left empty.
  `Hi {{name.firstName}},` becomes `Hi ,` for someone without a first name.
  Check your list, or write greetings that read well either way.
</Warning>

Twenty does not add your company's postal address for you. Add it to the bottom of the email, since US law requires it in marketing email.

## Part 4: Send a test

1. Click **Test** at the top of the campaign, or press `Cmd+K` and choose **Send Test Email**.
2. In **Recipient**, type your own address.
3. Click **Send test email**.

Check the layout, the links and the sender in your inbox, on desktop and on your phone.

<Note>
  Variables are left empty in test emails, because a test is not sent to a
  specific person. Your workspace can send 3 test emails per day.
</Note>

## Part 5: Send or schedule

1. Click **Send** at the top of the campaign, or press `Cmd+K` and choose **Send Campaign**.
2. Check the summary. **Recipients** shows how many people will receive the campaign, and why others are skipped, for example because they unsubscribed or have no email address.
3. Under **Delivery**, choose:
   - **Send now**: sending starts right away and cannot be undone.
   - **Send later**: pick a date and time in **Send at**.
4. Click **Send campaign** or **Schedule campaign**.

A campaign can reach up to 10,000 people. Split larger audiences into several lists.

<Note>
  A scheduled campaign is checked again when it sends. If no one on the list can
  receive it by then, or the sending domain is no longer verified, the campaign
  goes back to **Draft** instead of sending.
</Note>

## After you send

The campaign's **Status** shows where it is:

| Status               | Meaning                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Draft**            | Not sent yet. You can edit it.                                                       |
| **Scheduled**        | Waiting for its send time.                                                           |
| **Sending**          | Emails are going out.                                                                |
| **Sent**             | Every email was handed off.                                                          |
| **Sent with errors** | Some emails could not be sent, for example because the workspace ran out of credits. |
| **Canceled**         | Stopped before it finished.                                                          |

The **Home** tab shows the delivery counts: **Sent count**, **Delivered count**, **Bounced count**, **Complained count**, **Failed count** and **Skipped count**. The **Recipients** list shows who the campaign went to.

To send a similar campaign later, click **Duplicate** at the top of the campaign. It copies the email, list and settings into a new draft.

### Cancel a campaign

Click **Cancel** at the top of the campaign, or press `Cmd+K` and choose **Cancel Campaign**:

- **Scheduled**: nothing has gone out. Confirm with **Unschedule Campaign**. The campaign returns to your drafts, and you can edit or schedule it again.
- **Sending**: emails still waiting are stopped. Emails already sent cannot be recalled.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The From field has no addresses">
  **From** lists your email channels. Create an email channel first, then verify
  its domain. See [Set up a sending
  domain](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/set-up-a-sending-domain).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sending fails with No verified sending domain matches this from address">
  The **From** address is on a domain that is not verified yet. Finish the steps
  in [Set up a sending
  domain](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/set-up-a-sending-domain), or
  choose another address.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Send campaign is greyed out">
  The campaign needs a **From** address, a list, a subject and a body, and at
  least one person who can receive it. Check the **Recipients** summary for
  people who are skipped. With **Send later**, the send time must also be in the
  future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sending fails with This campaign cannot be sent">
  Check that the campaign has a **From** address, a list and a subject. Then
  check the variables: a variable that does not match a field on People blocks
  sending. Insert variables from **Variables** rather than typing them, and use
  `{{name.firstName}}`, not `{{firstName}}`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A scheduled campaign went back to Draft">
  When it was time to send, the campaign could not go out, for example because
  no one on the list could receive it or the domain was no longer verified. Fix
  the cause, then schedule it again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't find Campaigns">
  Email campaigns may not be turned on for your workspace yet. Contact us to
  enable them.
</Accordion>
