---
title: Create a Campaign with AI
description: Let the AI build your list and write the whole email, then review it and send.
---

Twenty's AI works directly with your CRM data, so it can do most of the work of a campaign for you. Describe who you want to reach and what you want to say. The AI finds the right people, builds the list and writes the email, layout and personalization included. You review the draft, send yourself a test, and click send.

<Note>
  The AI prepares drafts. It never sends a campaign on its own. Sending is
  always a step you take yourself.
</Note>

## What the AI can do

| Task                   | Example                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Find people**        | "Find everyone at software companies in Germany whose job title contains Head or VP."      |
| **Build a list**       | "Create a list called Webinar invitees and add those people to it."                        |
| **Write the campaign** | Subject line, body, sections, columns, buttons, images and raw HTML.                       |
| **Personalize**        | Adds variables like `{{name.firstName}}` so each person gets their own version.            |
| **Edit a draft**       | "Make it shorter", "Add a button linking to our pricing page", "Translate it into French". |

The AI works with your permissions. It can only see and change the records your role allows.

## Part 1: Open the AI

Press `@` anywhere in Twenty, or press `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows) and choose **Ask AI**.

## Part 2: Build your audience

Tell the AI who the campaign is for. Describe the people the way you would describe them to a colleague, using fields that exist in your workspace.

> Create a list called "Q4 product update" with everyone at companies that have more than 50 employees and are in the healthcare industry.

The AI searches your records, creates the list and adds the people it found. Open the list afterwards to check its members before you send.

<Warning>
  Only add people who agreed to receive your emails. A list built from everyone
  in your CRM will include people you have only exchanged emails with. See
  [Email campaign best
  practices](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/email-campaign-best-practices).
</Warning>

## Part 3: Write the campaign

Describe the email: its purpose, the key points, the tone, and any links or buttons.

> Write a campaign announcing our new reporting dashboard. Keep the subject under 50 characters. Start with a short, friendly intro that greets each person by first name, list three benefits in a two-column layout, and end with a "See what's new" button linking to https://example.com/reports.

The AI creates a draft campaign with the name, subject and body. Open it from **Campaigns** to see the result in the editor.

To change something, keep the conversation going. Open the campaign first, and the AI knows which one you mean:

- "Make the tone more casual."
- "Shorten the intro to two sentences."
- "Replace the button text with Book a demo."
- "Suggest three other subject lines."

You can also edit the draft yourself in the editor at any time.

## Part 4: Review and send

The AI writes the email, but you choose where it goes and when:

1. Open the campaign's **Email** tab.
2. Choose the **From** address, the list under **To** and, optionally, an **Unsubscribe topic**.
3. Read the email through. Check the facts, links and prices the AI wrote.
4. Click **Test** and send yourself a copy.
5. Click **Send**, then choose **Send now** or **Send later**.

Each step is covered in detail in [Send your first campaign](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/send-your-first-campaign).

## Tips for good prompts

- **Name the fields you care about.** "Contacts whose city is Paris" works better than "my French contacts".
- **Give the facts.** The AI does not know your launch date, prices or offer unless you tell it or they are in your CRM.
- **Ask for structure.** Mention sections, columns, bullet points or buttons if you want them.
- **Paste your own HTML** if you already have a design, and ask the AI to fill in the content.
- **Check the list size** before sending. Ask "How many people are in the Q4 product update list?"

## Limitations

- The AI can only edit campaigns that are still **Draft**.
- Unsubscribe topics and the unsubscribe list are managed in **Settings**, not through the AI. See [Manage unsubscribes and topics](/user-guide/email-campaigns/how-tos/manage-unsubscribes-and-topics).
- Like any AI, it can get facts wrong. Always read the email before you send it.
