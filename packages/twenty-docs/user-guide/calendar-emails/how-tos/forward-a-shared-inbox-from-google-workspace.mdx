---
title: Forward a Shared Inbox from Google Workspace
description: Send mail from a Google Group into a Twenty email channel.
---

An email channel gives your workspace a shared address, like `sales@yourcompany.com`. Mail sent to that address has to reach Twenty somehow.

On Google Workspace that address is almost always a **Google Group**. You connect it by adding Twenty's private forwarding address as a member of the group. Every message the group receives then gets copied to Twenty, and your team keeps getting it exactly as before.

<Note>
  This only works going forward. Twenty will not see mail the group received
  before you set this up.
</Note>

## Before you start

You need two things:

- **Access**: you must own the group, or be a Super Admin or Groups Admin in Google Workspace.
- **A group that allows outsiders**: Twenty's forwarding address is an external address, so the group has to accept external members and accept mail from outside your company. Part 2 covers both.

## Part 1: Create the channel in Twenty

1. Go to **Settings → Communication**.
2. Click **Add email channel**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/03-twenty-add-email-channel.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

3. In **Source Email Address**, type the group address, for example `sales@yourcompany.com`.
4. In **Display Name**, type the name recipients see.
5. Click **Save**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/04-twenty-new-channel-form.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

6. Twenty now shows a **Forwarding address**. It looks like `ch_2cd282d193b5@inbound.twenty.com`. Click **Copy**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/05-twenty-forwarding-address.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

<Note>
  Each channel gets its own forwarding address. Never point two groups at the
  same one, or their mail will land in the same place.
</Note>

## Part 2: Check the group's settings

Two settings decide whether any of this works. Get them right first and the rest takes a minute.

1. Open [Google Groups](https://groups.google.com) and click your group.
2. Click **Group settings** in the left sidebar.
3. Find **External members** and turn **Allow external members** on.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-03-allow-external-members.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

Google ships this turned off for the whole organisation by default, so expect it to be locked the first time. If you see the blue notice shown above, only a Workspace admin can unlock it, by allowing group owners to add external members org-wide. Ask them, then come back.

4. Scroll down to **Who can post** and drag the slider all the way to the right, onto the globe. That is **Anyone on the web**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-04-who-can-post.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

Without this, mail from outside your company never reaches the group, so it never reaches Twenty either. This one is also blocked org-wide by default, and again only an admin can open it up.

5. Click **Save changes**.

<Warning>
  If the group carries Google's **Security** label, stop here. Google does not
  allow non-Google accounts in a security group, and the label is permanent once
  applied. Create a new group without it.
</Warning>

## Part 3: Add Twenty to the group

1. Click **Members** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add members**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-02-members-add-button.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

3. Paste the forwarding address you copied from Twenty into **Group members**.
4. Leave **Subscription** on **Each email**. This is what makes Google send every message through.
5. Leave **Directly add members** ticked, so Google adds it instead of emailing an invitation nobody can accept.
6. Click **Add members**.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-05-add-forwarding-address.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

That is the whole setup. Twenty is now just another member of the group.

## No group yet?

1. In [Google Groups](https://groups.google.com), click **Create group**.
2. Fill in the **Group name** and the **Group email** prefix.

<img
  src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-01-create-group.png"
  style={{ width: '100%' }}
/>

3. Click **Next** twice, then **Create group**.
4. Now do Part 2 and Part 3.

## Part 4: Test it

Send an email from an outside address (a personal Gmail works) to `sales@yourcompany.com`. It should show up in Twenty within a minute or two.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="An error occurred: 1 user is outside of your organization">
  The group does not accept external members. Go back to Part 2, step 3. If the
  setting is locked, only a Workspace admin can unlock it.

  <img
    src="/images/user-guide/emails/inbound-channels/google-06-external-member-error.png"
    style={{ width: '100%' }}
  />
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The group gets mail but Twenty does not">
  Open the member in the group and check its subscription is **Each email**, not
  **No email** or **Digest**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Colleagues' emails arrive but customers' emails do not">
  **Who can post** is not set to **Anyone on the web**. Part 2, step 4.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The channel receives mail but cannot send">
  Receiving and sending are separate. Sending needs your domain verified under
  the channel's **Sending domain** section, which means adding the DNS records
  Twenty lists there.
</Accordion>

Using a different provider? See [Microsoft 365](/user-guide/calendar-emails/how-tos/forward-a-shared-inbox-from-microsoft-365) or [any other mail provider](/user-guide/calendar-emails/how-tos/forward-a-shared-inbox-from-any-mail-provider).
