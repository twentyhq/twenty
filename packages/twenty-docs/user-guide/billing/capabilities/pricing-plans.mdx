## Overview

Twenty offers flexible pricing to fit teams of all sizes, whether you prefer cloud hosting or self-hosting. See current prices on [twenty.com/pricing](https://twenty.com/pricing).

## Cloud Plans

### Pro (Cloud)
For one team:
- All core CRM features
- Email and calendar sync
- Workflows and automations
- **Admin** and **Member** roles
- Personal AI usage: about 100 AI tasks per member per week
- 1 workspace credit per user per month
- Email and chat support

<Note>
Premium features (custom roles, row-level permissions, SSO and more) are not included in the Pro plan.
</Note>

### Organization (Cloud)
For several teams that need more AI and control:
- Everything in Pro
- 5x more personal AI usage: about 500 AI tasks per member per week
- 4 workspace credits per user per month
- **Premium features**: custom roles, row-level permissions, SSO and more
- Priority support

See [Credits](/user-guide/billing/capabilities/credits) for how personal AI usage and workspace credits work.

### Legacy Cloud Plans
Subscriptions that started before the current plans keep their terms, and the Plans page marks them **Legacy**:
- **Pro (legacy)**: custom roles with field-level permissions, email and chat support
- **Organization (legacy)**: everything in legacy Pro, plus the Premium features and priority support
- Both include 5 workspace credits per month (50 per year on yearly billing) and no personal AI usage

## Self-Hosted Plans

### Free (Self-Hosted)
Host Twenty on your own infrastructure at no cost:
- All Pro features included, plus custom roles
- Community support via Discord
- Full control over your data

### Organization (Self-Hosted)
For teams who need premium features while self-hosting:
- Everything in Free
- **Premium features**: row-level permissions, SSO and more
- Twenty team support
- No requirement to publish custom code as open-source before distributing

## Premium Features

Premium features are only available on the Organization plans (Cloud or Self-Hosted):
- **Custom roles**: Roles beyond Admin and Member, with object and field permissions (cloud only, self-hosted instances include them on every plan)
- **Row-level permissions**: Fine-grained access control at the record level
- **SSO integration**: Single Sign-On with your identity provider
- **Audit logs**: Check what each workspace member has been doing
- **Usage limits for members, API keys and apps**: Cap what each of them can spend
- **Custom domain**: Serve your workspace from your own domain (cloud only)
- **Unlimited workspaces**: Create as many workspaces as you want (self-hosted only)
- **Private source code**: No requirement to publish changes to a publicly available repository (self-hosted only)

## Switching Plans

### Upgrade to Organization
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plans**
2. Click **Switch to Organization**
3. Confirm your upgrade

### Downgrade to Pro
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plans**
2. Click **Switch to Pro**
3. Confirm your downgrade

### Switch to Yearly Billing
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade to Yearly**
3. Save with annual billing

### Switch to Monthly Billing
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Downgrade to Monthly**
3. Save with monthly billing

## Get an Organization Key

The Organization (Self-Hosted) plan runs on your own infrastructure. To get started, [install Twenty with Docker Compose](/developers/self-host/capabilities/docker-compose), then follow the steps below to activate your Organization license.

If you already have a self-hosted Twenty instance, you can get your Organization key directly from its settings:

1. Go to **Settings → Admin Panel → Organization**

<img src="/images/user-guide/billing/organization-key.png" alt="Organization key" />

2. Click **Get Organization Key**
3. When you are redirected to Stripe, enter your payment details and confirm
4. When your Organization key is displayed, paste it into the Organization settings page and activate the Organization license
