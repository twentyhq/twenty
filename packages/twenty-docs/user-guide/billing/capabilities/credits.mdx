## What are credits?

Credits are a single balance that powers everything we run on your behalf that has a real cost: **workflow automations**, **AI agents**, and the **AI chat**. Instead of juggling separate quotas for "automation runs" and "AI tokens," you have one simple balance to keep an eye on.

**1 credit = \$1 of usage.** A credit is just a dollar of work, so your balance is easy to reason about — no abstract units to convert.

## How far does a credit go?

Cost scales with the work. Everyday automation and quick AI messages cost a tiny fraction of a credit; large, multi-step AI agent tasks cost more. Here's the rough shape:

| What you do | Roughly costs | What that means |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Run a standard workflow step (search, create, or update records) | \$0.0001 | **10,000 runs per credit** |
| Trigger an app's logic function | \$0.0001 + \$0.0001 per second of runtime | **Thousands of runs per credit** |
| Call an external API or run a code node | A small fraction of a credit | Thousands per credit |
| Send a quick AI chat message or simple AI step | A fraction of a cent | Hundreds per credit |
| Run a large AI agent task (e.g. "configure 10 workspace objects") | Can run to **~\$1 or more** | A few per credit |
| Run an AI web search | A fixed per-search cost | Hundreds of searches per credit |

<Note>
For most teams, standard automations are effectively free — you'll only make a noticeable dent in your balance when you lean on AI features or run heavy code nodes. That's by design: the included credits are meant to let you build without watching a meter.
</Note>

<Note>
Not every app charges for its runs. Call Recorder, Last Contact, Fathom and Granola react to every email, calendar event and recording imported into your workspace, and Slack runs on every Slack event its bot subscribes to, so their logic function runs are free. You are still charged for the metered work they do, such as recorded call minutes or the AI credits behind Slack assistant answers.
</Note>

## How logic function runs are metered

Logic function pricing mirrors AWS Lambda: a flat charge per invocation plus a duration charge for the time your code actually runs, billed per millisecond.

- **Per invocation**: \$0.0001
- **Per duration**: \$0.0001 per second of runtime (\$0.0000001 per millisecond)

A typical function that finishes in under a second costs at most double the flat invocation charge. Long-running functions pay for the compute they use: a function running for a full minute adds \$0.006, and one hitting the 15-minute timeout adds \$0.09. A function that times out is billed for its full elapsed time, just like on AWS.

For apps with [server routes](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#owner-workspace-costs-and-availability), the resolver runs against the app owner's workspace balance. A target function dispatched to another workspace is billed separately to that workspace.

## How AI usage is metered

When an action calls an AI model, we measure the exact tokens used and price them at **the model provider's published rates**, then convert that dollar amount straight into credits — so what you spend tracks the real cost of the work, not a marked-up internal rate. A short chat message costs a fraction of a cent. A large agent task — say, configuring 10 workspace objects — makes many model calls over a growing context, so it can add up to a dollar or more. You're billed for what you actually use, down to the token.

Cost varies by model: a lightweight model is dramatically cheaper per message than a top-tier reasoning model. You can see the per-action breakdown — including which model was used — in **Settings → Billing**.

## What's included

Every subscription includes **workspace credits**, shared by the whole workspace. The amount depends on your plan and your number of users:

| Plan | Included Credits | Equivalent Usage |
|------|------------------|------------------|
| Pro | 1 per user per month | ~\$1 of usage per user each month |
| Organization | 4 per user per month | ~\$4 of usage per user each month |
| Legacy plans | 5 per month, or 50 per year on yearly billing | ~\$5 of usage each month |

On yearly billing, the allowance covers the whole year: 12 credits per user on Pro and 48 on Organization.

These credits are designed to cover everyday automation comfortably. One credit covers about 10,000 standard workflow steps or hundreds of quick AI messages, though a handful of large, multi-step agent tasks can use them up faster. You can add a credit pack at any time (see below).

## Personal AI usage

On the Pro and Organization plans, each member also gets a weekly AI allowance of their own. AI chat and web search use it before workspace credits:

| Plan | Personal AI Usage |
|------|-------------------|
| Pro | About 100 AI tasks per member per week |
| Organization | About 500 AI tasks per member per week |

An AI task is one chat request with the default model; smarter models use more. The allowance renews every Monday at 00:00 UTC. AI agents in workflows always run on workspace credits.

When a member runs out, their chats continue on workspace credits until the allowance renews. Members with the **Billing** settings permission can make those chats stop instead, under **Settings → Billing**. Legacy plans have no personal AI usage, so AI chat runs on workspace credits.

## Credit rollover

Included credits on the Pro and Organization plans are spent first and renew each billing period. They don't roll over.

Unused credits from a credit pack, and the included credits of legacy plans, roll over automatically to the next billing period.

- **Cap**: Rollover is capped at one period's allocation, so these credits never add up to more than twice what a period provides.
- **Visibility**: When you have rollover credits, they appear as a separate **Rollover Credits** line in **Settings → Billing**, alongside your **Total Available** balance.

## Monitoring usage

Keep track of your balance and where it's going:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. See your **remaining credits** and **usage for the current period**
3. Drill into the breakdown to see which workflows and AI actions are consuming credits — and which models AI actions used

Reviewing this regularly makes it easy to spot a heavy workflow early and decide whether to optimize it or top up.

## Need more credits?

If you run out, or want a buffer before a big automation push, you can add a credit pack at any time. Packs cost \$1 per credit, from \$20 per month, and renew with your subscription:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click the **Increase** button
3. Select the amount you need and accept the changes

Purchased credits are added to your available balance immediately.
