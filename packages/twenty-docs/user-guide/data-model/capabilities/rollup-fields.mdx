title: Rollup Fields
description: Count or calculate over the records linked to a record.
---

## What Is a Rollup Field?

A rollup field summarizes the records behind one of an object's relations. On Companies, for example, a rollup can show the number of open opportunities of each company, the date of its last meeting, or the total estimate of its tasks.

A rollup is a field like any other: it shows in tables and record pages, and you can filter and sort views by it. You cannot edit it: Twenty keeps it up to date.

<Note>
  Rollup fields are being rolled out and may not be enabled on your workspace yet.
</Note>

## Create a Rollup Field

1. Go to **Settings → Data Model** and select the object that should show the result, such as Companies.
2. Click **+ New Field** and choose **Rollup**.
3. Under **Related records**, pick the relation to summarize, such as Opportunities.
4. Under **Calculation**, pick what to compute:
   - **Count**: the number of related records.
   - **Sum** and **Average**: of a number field.
   - **Min** and **Max**: of a number, date or date and time field.
5. For every calculation but Count, pick the **Field** of the related records to calculate on.
6. Optionally, add **Filters** to only count or calculate on some related records, such as opportunities whose stage is not Customer.
7. Click **Save**.

Twenty computes the field for every existing record in the background. Large objects can take a few minutes.

## How Rollups Stay Up to Date

A rollup is recalculated a few seconds after a related record is created, edited, moved to another record, deleted or restored. The new value appears in open tabs without reloading.

A record without any related record shows 0 for a count and an empty value for the other calculations. Deleted records are never counted.

Twenty also recalculates every rollup once a night, which catches changes made outside the app, such as some imports.

## Limits

- A rollup goes through a relation that lists several records. To show a value of a single related record, add a lookup column instead.
- Filters compare to fixed values. Filters relative to today, such as "in the past 30 days", are not available yet.
- A rollup cannot calculate on currency fields yet.
- An object can have up to 10 rollup fields.
- A rollup follows the permissions of the field itself. It counts every related record, including the ones a person cannot see.

## When a Field It Uses Is Deleted

Deleting the relation, the field a rollup calculates on, or a field one of its filters uses also deletes the rollup. Renaming a select option updates the rollup's filters automatically.
