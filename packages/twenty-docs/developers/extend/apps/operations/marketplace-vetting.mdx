## How vetting works

<Warning>
  **Work in progress.** This page and its requirements may evolve. Third-party
  vetting is not available yet. See
  [Publishing](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#marketplace-visibility-and-vetting)
  for current marketplace behavior.
</Warning>

Vetting applies to an exact app version and package. Each update needs review and
approval from Twenty. Vetted apps may be reassessed as dependencies or services
evolve.

## Vetting checklist

All applicable items must pass. We recommend using an agent to self-evaluate
each criterion with evidence and required fixes before submitting.

- [ ] **Publisher.** [Claim your app](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#claiming-your-app)
  and [publish with provenance](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#ci-publishing).
- [ ] **Security.** Pass Twenty's internal security review and resolve blocking
  findings.
- [ ] **Permissions.** Request only the
  [permissions](/developers/extend/apps/config/roles) needed for the app's features.
- [ ] **Functionality.** Setup and core workflows are easy to follow and work as
  advertised, without crashes, lost work, or duplicate records. No placeholder
  features or broken controls.
- [ ] **Performance.** Respect shared API/job rate limits, handle throttling, and
  follow the
  [app development good practices](/developers/extend/apps/getting-started/good-practices).
- [ ] **Usefulness.** Complete a meaningful CRM task for a broader audience than
  one customer's setup. Small connectors and headless automations qualify.
- [ ] **Native experience.** Reuse native objects, views, and workflows where appropriate.
  If the app has a UI, use
  [`twenty-ui`](/developers/extend/apps/layout/front-components#using-twenty-ui-components)
  and match Twenty's layouts, interactions, and accessibility conventions.
- [ ] **Privacy.** Explain what data leaves the workspace, where it goes and why,
  including AI providers, how long it is kept, and how it can be deleted.
- [ ] **Maintenance and compatibility.** Declare supported Twenty versions, keep
  entity identifiers stable, and fix critical bugs and broken integrations over
  time.
- [ ] **Logo and gallery.** Provide an original, recognizable logo and polished
  images of the actual app. Follow the
  [image guidelines](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#marketplace-metadata).
- [ ] **Listing and README.** Accurately describe features and affiliations in
  the name, description, and screenshots. Keep the README short, well-formatted,
  and focused on the app's value.
- [ ] **Pricing.** Clearly state whether the app is free or paid, plan prices,
  billing frequency, and any required paid third-party services.
- [ ] **Documentation and support.** Provide setup instructions, a working example,
  and a support contact. If using shared routes, complete
  [setup](/developers/extend/apps/operations/publishing#setting-up-server-routes)
  and verify events reach the correct workspace.
